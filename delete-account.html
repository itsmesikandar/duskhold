Delete your Duskhold account

Last updated: 21 October 2026

You sign in to Duskhold with Google. You can delete that account at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Duskhold and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, for example because you uninstalled it, email appsdevsphere@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with and ask to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account on Supabase, Inc., which includes your name, email address, profile picture, Google account ID and sign-in records.

What stays

  • Your blocks and settings, which are on your phone only. Uninstall Duskhold or clear its data to remove them.
  • Your purchase history. Google Play and RevenueCat keep it to run your subscription. To stop a subscription, cancel it in Google Play.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports in Firebase. They are not linked to your name or email.
